# Privacy policy - Courier Copilot

**Draft.** The founder and the lawyer have to read this before the app goes
live (the legal review is in `docs/status/FOUNDER_TODO.md`). The privacy officer owns
it under the NZ Privacy Act 2020.

Last updated: 2026-10-21 (version 0.1.0)

## Who we are

Courier Copilot is an app for independent parcel couriers in New Zealand. The
privacy contact is privacy@courier.copilot.nz.

## What the app collects

The app collects what a delivery run needs:

- **Your account**: the name, the email address and the password (hashed) of
  your account.
- **Your runs**: the stops, the addresses, the order, the times, the status of
  every stop and the proof of delivery (a photo, a signature or a voice note).
- **Your location**: while a run is active, so the map, the stop list and the
  arrival times stay right, including with the screen off.
- **Your voice**: the voice commands and the voice assistant. The audio is
  processed on the phone and on our server while the command is being handled.
  It is not stored unless you ask for it.
- **The door notes and the hazards**: the gate code, the side gate, the dog and
  the parking note you leave for a door.
- **The device**: the model, the system version and the app version, for the
  crash reports and the support.

## Why we collect it

To run the delivery: to show the route, to record what happened at every stop,
to pay the summary at the end of the day and to fix an address when the courier
types it wrong. The location is only collected while a run is active.

## Who sees it

Nobody outside the app and the service that runs it, except:

- the hosting provider, which stores the data in New Zealand and Australia,
- the payment provider, when a team plan is paid for (it never sees the delivery
  data),
- the analytics provider, which only sees anonymous events (a run was started, a
  stop was marked), never the address, the name or the location.

We do not sell the data, and we do not share it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

The run data stays until you delete the run or the account. The account data is
deleted within 30 days of the request. The crash reports are kept for 90 days.

## Your choices

- delete a run and its photos at any time,
- delete the account in the app (Settings, Delete account) or on the web at
  https://courier.copilot.nz/delete-account,
- ask for a copy of your data at privacy@courier.copilot.nz.

## The location, in plain words

The app asks for the location because a courier needs the map and the stop list
while driving, with the phone in the pocket. The disclosure is shown in the app
before the permission is asked for. The location stops when the run ends.

## Changes

We will put the date at the top of this policy when it changes. If a change
matters for your data, the app tells you.

## The privacy principle we work to

Only the personal information we need, only for the reason we need it, only for
as long as we need it, and visible to the person it belongs to.
