**Franchise Economics (Aramex example):**
- Franchise purchase: $300K–$600K NZD
- Weekly franchise fee: $800–$1,200
- Driver pay: $0.80–$1.20 per parcel + fuel surcharge
- Driver earnings: $1,200–$2,000/week gross
- **Pain point:** Franchisees *pay* for Blu but drivers hate it → franchisees would pay for better driver tool

## 7. Regulatory & Privacy Landscape (NZ)

| Regulation | Impact on Courier Copilot |
|------------|---------------------------|
| **Privacy Act 2020** | Address data = personal info. Must: minimize collection, encrypt, allow deletion, NZ data residency |
| **Contractor vs Employee** | Govt cracking down on "sham contracting" — tools that help contractors *prove* independence (KM log, earnings) are valuable |
| **GST on digital services** | Stripe Tax handles automatically (15% GST on $35 = $5.25) |
| **Health & Safety at Work Act** | Hands-free voice = compliance asset (no phone handling while driving) |
| **NZTA Work Time Rules** | Heavy vehicle drivers need logbooks — we don't target this segment yet |

**Data Residency Requirement:** All driver/customer data must stay in **NZ or Australia (Sydney)**. Supabase Sydney / AWS Sydney / Fly.io Sydney satisfy this.

## 10. Risks to Market Assumptions

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Aramex bans 3rd party apps | Medium | High (280 drivers locked) | Build "alongside" value; franchisee pressure |
| NZ Post mandates internal app | Low | Medium | Contractor model protects choice |
| Spoke adds voice + memory | Medium | High | Our moat = compounding memory + NZ data |
| Contractor reclassification → employees | Medium | Low (employees get tools free) | Pivot to B2B dispatch |
| Recession → fewer parcels | Low | Medium | Contractors *need* efficiency more |
